5. Methodological Limitations & Disclaimer

Academic & Methodological Notice: This report was generated from a cross-sectional observational dataset comprising 1,338 records. 1. Association ≠ Causation: Group-level differences in insurance charges reflect observed correlation and cannot establish direct physiological or biological causation without randomized experimental controls. 2. Confounding Variables: Multi-factor interactions (such as age, body mass index, and geographic region) contribute substantially to individual medical costs. 3. Empirical Estimates: Subgroup averages describe the historical sample distribution and do not constitute an individualized clinical prediction or actuarial risk rating.
